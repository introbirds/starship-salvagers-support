# Starship Salvagers

Official support information and privacy policy for Starship Salvagers, a space salvage game for Android and iOS.

## Support

For help, bug reports, or feedback, open an issue in this repository’s **Issues** tab.

When reporting a problem, please include:

- App version
- Device model and Android or iOS version
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- What you expected and what happened
- A screenshot, if useful

Reports are public. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

## Privacy Policy

Read the [Starship Salvagers Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for information about how the app handles data.
