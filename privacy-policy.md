# Privacy Policy for Starship Salvagers

**Last updated: 11 October 2026**

Introbirds Pte. Ltd. (“we”, “us”, or “our”) develops Starship Salvagers. This policy explains how the current version of the game handles information, and what happens when you contact support or visit our support website.

## Information handled by the game

The current version of Starship Salvagers operates locally on your device. It does not require an account or automatically collect or transmit personal information to us.

The game does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, automated crash-reporting services, cloud saves, or online purchase processing.

## Local game data

The game stores information on your device so that you can resume playing and retain your preferences. This includes game progress, unlocks, gameplay records, tutorial progress, and settings.

The game also maintains local recovery saves. This information is used to provide gameplay and restore saved progress. It is not automatically uploaded to us.

If you use the manual backup feature, the game exports a save file to a location you select. Importing a backup reads the file you select to restore game data. We do not automatically receive these files.

Your device or operating system may separately back up app data according to your device settings. Files you place in a cloud-synced location are subject to that service’s privacy practices.

## Information you provide to support

If you email **support@introbirds.com**, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide, such as your name, device details, screenshots, or save files.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, investigate problems, and provide support. Our email service provider processes messages to deliver and store these communications.

Please provide only information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

If you submit a GitHub issue, your report and associated GitHub profile information may be publicly visible. For private enquiries, contact us by email.

## Sharing information

The current game does not automatically share your local game data with us or third parties.

We do not sell information you provide to support or use it for advertising. Support information may be processed by service providers needed to handle your enquiry, or disclosed where required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. On Android, you can remove app data through your device’s settings. Deleting the app generally removes its local app data, subject to your operating system’s behaviour.

Exported save files and copies retained by device backups or cloud storage may remain separately. You can delete those copies through the relevant file manager, backup settings, or storage service.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, investigate related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to support by emailing **support@introbirds.com**. Applicable legal requirements may limit deletion in some circumstances.

## Support website

Our support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Use of GitHub, including submitting issues, is subject to the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children’s privacy

The current game does not automatically collect personal information from players, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support enquiry, contact us so that we can review and address it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or our information-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
